🔐 Decentralized Encrypted Storage

SecureChain's storage solution combines **AES-256 military-grade encryption** with the **immutability of blockchain**, guaranteeing data integrity, censorship resistance, and complete user control over decryption keys. Your data, locked in the chain.

⚙️ SecureChain Storage Process (Three-Step Security)

1. Client-Side Encryption

Data is encrypted locally using the user's private key and **AES-256**. The data leaves your device already scrambled.

2. Decentralized Sharding

The encrypted data is split into multiple shards and stored across a **decentralized network of nodes**, preventing single points of failure.

3. Blockchain Key & Hash Validation

Only the **storage hash and the access metadata** are recorded on the ledger, validating the data's integrity and securing the decryption key pointer.

🚀 Unmatched Security & Compliance

  • ✅ Immutability: Once stored, data cannot be deleted or tampered with, meeting regulatory requirements.
  • ✅ Proven Algorithms: Uses industry-standard **AES-256** for encryption and **SHA-256** for hashing.
  • ✅ Enhanced Privacy: True **Zero-Knowledge** architecture—SecureChain nodes cannot read your files.
  • ✅ Disaster Recovery: Built-in data redundancy across the decentralized network ensures near-zero downtime and loss.
