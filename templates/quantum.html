From Classical to Quantum-Resistant Cryptography

The Quantum Computing Threat

Traditional encryption methods like RSA and ECC rely on mathematical problems that are difficult for classical computers but vulnerable to quantum algorithms.

  • Shor's Algorithm: Can factor large numbers exponentially faster, breaking RSA encryption
  • Grover's Algorithm: Provides quadratic speedup for database search problems
  • Current Impact: Most public-key cryptography used today becomes vulnerable

Lattice-Based Solutions

SecureChain adopts NIST-standardized lattice-based cryptography, which uses mathematical problems that remain hard for both classical and quantum computers.

  • Learning With Errors (LWE): Basis for many post-quantum schemes
  • Module-LWE: Efficient variant used in CRYSTALS-Kyber
  • Short Integer Solution (SIS): Foundation for signature schemes like Dilithium
