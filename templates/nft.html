📜 Certification & Validation Flow

The four-stage process guarantees non-repudiation and global verification of any document.

Document Certification Process Flow

📄

Document Creation

Issuer creates the original certificate document

🔗

Hashing & Timestamping

SHA-256 hash recorded on blockchain

🖼️

NFT Minting

NFT created with metadata linked to document hash

🔐

Ownership Transfer

NFT transferred to recipient's wallet

🔗

1. Hashing & Timestamping

The digital document is hashed (SHA-256). This unique fingerprint is recorded on the blockchain, creating an immutable timestamp.

🖼️

2. NFT Minting

A unique NFT is minted, linking its metadata to the document's hash and the issuing authority's wallet address.

🔐

3. Ownership Transfer

The NFT is transferred to the rightful recipient (student, employee, etc.), granting immutable proof of ownership and authenticity.

✅

4. Instant Verification

Any party can instantly verify the NFT against the blockchain to confirm the document's integrity and issuer identity.
